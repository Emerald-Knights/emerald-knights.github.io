Who We Are

Background

Founded in 2015, the Emerald Knights is a FIRST® Tech Challenge team focused on rigorous engineering and gracious professionalism. We design, prototype, and code competitive robots while mentoring younger students and serving our community.

Mission

Every year, we compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge, a robotics competition designed for high school students to build robots that complete complex tasks. The game encourages not only technical learning, but also collaboration, sportsmanship, and fun.

Experience

Heralding from Livingston, NJ, our team has been active for over 10 years. We have reached the World Championships in Detroit twice, and we strive to pass our knowledge to new members and our community every season.
