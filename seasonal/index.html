Mock Season

During the summer, our leads host intensive training bootcamps. New members learn the fundamentals of **Java programming**, **Fusion 360 CAD modeling**, and precision mechanical assembly to hit the ground running in September.

Cadet Mentorship

Our team legacy is built on mentorship. Our off-season program pairs veteran members with local FLL teams and middle school students, fostering a passion for robotics and ensuring a sustainable pipeline of talent.
